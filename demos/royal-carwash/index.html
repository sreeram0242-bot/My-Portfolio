THE ROYAL PROCESS

FROM ARRIVAL TO
SHOWROOM SHINE

A structured 5-step process — so every vehicle leaves in its best possible condition, every single time.

01

Check-In & Inspection

Vehicle is registered, photographed, and your chosen service package is confirmed. Any existing damage is noted before we begin.

02

Snow Foam Pre-Soak

PH-neutral snow foam is applied and allowed to dwell, lifting surface contaminants without touching the paint — protecting your clear coat from day one.

03

High-Pressure Wash

Foam is rinsed off with precision high-pressure water, including underbody and wheel arches — blasting away road grime and hidden corrosive debris.

04

Detail & Polish

Our technicians apply the selected finish — ceramic coating, interior detailing, or machine polish — with precision microfiber and specialist tools.

05

Quality Check & Handover

A final inspection confirms the finish meets Royal standards. You receive an SMS notification and pick up a showroom-clean vehicle — ready to roll.
